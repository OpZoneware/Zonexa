Metric Definitions

Rule-based statuses — calculated, never opinion-based.

Fresh: updated within 3 days Aging: 4–6 days old Stale: more than 6 days old Watchlist: 1–3 days overdue or beyond SLA Escalate: more than 3 days overdue or beyond SLA
